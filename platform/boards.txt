# Tools > USB-PD Power: highest voltage asked of a USB-PD charger. VBUS feeds the
# on-board regulator and the VIN pin, so VIN follows it. Sets the STUSB4500's
# working registers at start-up (variant.cpp), never its NVM; until then the chip
# uses its NVM, set to 5 V only on Phyduino boards (tests/sketches/usbpd_nvm).
